# C2: physical-feedback learning of conditional reconstruction

C2 learns conditional crystal proposals and their relative verification from measured structural outcomes. Frozen F supplies a continuous reference derived from a C1 draft. A geometry-conditioned DLM proposes masked alternatives; physical evaluation of the reference and executed alternatives is compiled into supervision, allowing the conditional model to reuse this experience on subsequent requests.

[Full story and mathematics](../PHYSICAL_FEEDBACK_MASTER_STORY_ZH.md) · [Implementation and reproduction](../keep-edit-implementation.md) · [Detailed Chinese notes](../../private/06_C2_ZH.md)

## From structural outcomes to model learning

1. Warm up conditional reconstruction on known corruptions of training crystals.
2. Obtain continuous references using B0+C1 and frozen F on saved training Plans.
3. Generate conditional candidates and execute patches that preserve unchanged continuous fields.
4. Physically evaluate the references and executed candidates under the same protocol.
5. Compile content/scope/KEEP supervision and reliable relative outcome pairs.
6. Fit the conditional editor, then mix in a finite-candidate physical teacher for a light update.
7. Use the final editor's features to fit its relative value; fit geometric risk from measured invalid/non-converged outcomes.

The feedback updates conditional reconstruction parameters. The separate B0+C1 assets used for initial raw generation are not automatically updated by this workflow.

## A finite-candidate physical teacher

Retain ordered records $b_k=(\hat y^{(k)},a_k,C_k)$, including KEEP $C_0=S$. Physical utility acts on the executed structure $C_k$; model features also use proposal tokens and action metadata. Records are not deduplicated by geometry.

$$
A_k=u(C_k)-u(S),\qquad
q_T(k\mid S)=\frac{p_0(k)\exp(A_k/\beta)}
{\sum_jp_0(j)\exp(A_j/\beta)}.
$$

With a uniform reference over retained records, this solves

$$
\max_q\;\mathbb E_q[A]-\beta\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p_0).
$$

The retained recipe mixes teacher-sampled targets with original editor supervision at fraction 0.25 and temperature 0.1. Typed content reconstruction, reference KL and decision supervision fit the conditional DLM. The light update runs for one epoch at LoRA LR `1e-6` after the eight-epoch editor stage. This is physical preference-guided conditional fitting, not an exact trajectory DPO loss or an implemented multi-round online loop.

## Verification follows actual execution

The model sees the reference's token view and the evolving proposal; execution changes only fields with actual numerical changes:

$$
C_k=\operatorname{Patch}(S,Q(S),\hat y^{(k)}).
$$

Physical labels correspond to this complete executed structure, including preserved continuous fields. They cannot automatically be assigned to a fully token-decoded structure used by a different learner.

## A learned relative verifier

For full candidate records:

$$
\Delta v_\phi(S,b_k)=v_\phi(S,b_k)-v_\phi(S,b_0),\qquad
J_0(b_k)=2\Delta v_{NS}(b_k)+\Delta v_{NMS}(b_k).
$$

KEEP has zero relative value. `NS` and `NMS` are per-structure novelty/stability targets; uniqueness is recomputed on the final panel. Value fitting uses outcome differences, anchoring and within-source ranking, with a physical-feature auxiliary teacher used only offline. The deployed value consumes final-editor and geometry features.

The one-revision variant reopens a bounded set of changed fields in an eligible single-site, lattice-preserving candidate. It uses completed context and predicted risk, produces at most one extra candidate, and enters risk-adjusted selection only after its intervention gates pass. Full runtime rules and result attribution are recorded in the [implementation guide](../keep-edit-implementation.md).
